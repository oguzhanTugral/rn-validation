Disagreements: positions at which the engines differ

Positions at which all three engines emit a label and at least one of them, after normalisation, writes something different. These are the rows that tell the engines apart. Selecting a row opens the corresponding bar of the score.

Positions at which an engine emits no label are not listed here. A missing label is a gap in coverage, not a disagreement, and counting it as one would distort the agreement rate.
Preliminary results. These are first results. After detailed validation, the final accuracy figures may change by up to ±5 percentage points for AnalysisGNN and AugmentedNet and by about ±1 point for musWM. See Ratings for how the margin was estimated.
