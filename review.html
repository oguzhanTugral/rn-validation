Training blind review

Training round. The first sample, kept so the rating can be practised and its questions improved. Its answers are kept apart and are not the results the site reports; the rating that counts is the Blind review.

Rate, against the score, what the analysers read at a position. Part 1 checks musWM’s notes, root and bass with no Roman numeral in sight. Part 2 then asks which Roman numeral(s) are acceptable, choosing among the three analysers’ answers shown without names; who gave which is revealed only after you confirm.

How to rate
  1. Each position is one musWM reading, from Start to End. In the score, blue notes are in musWM’s list, orange notes are tensions the key contains, red ones are chromatic tensions; both sound in the span but are not in the list, and teal notes were already sounding before Start.
  2. Part 1. Find the chord at Start (passing, neighbour, suspended and ornamental notes are not chord notes) and answer the four questions below. Choose Unclear whenever you cannot decide.
  3. Part 2. Once the four are answered, the Roman-numeral question opens and the four lock. It shows the key and the options: the three analysers’ Roman numerals without names (identical ones merged), up to two alternatives made by rule, None of these (write the right one) and Unclear. Tick all acceptable answers, then confirm. The page then shows who gave each option and which analysers your answer counts as correct.
  4. Changing your mind. The four answers of Part 1 can be undone at any time. A Roman-numeral answer can be undone once per position after the reveal; your first answer stays on record.
NotesExactly = the list is only the chord notes. All + extra = every chord note is there, plus a passing/neighbour note or a note that does not sound. Missing = a chord note is not in the list — an orange note that is a chord note of the chord at Start. Spelling is ignored (G♭ = F♯; say so in the note).
RootIs this the root of the chord at Start?
BassIs this the lowest chord note at Start? If a lower note enters later in the span (broken chord, moving bass), choose Unclear and write “bass X→Y”.
SpanOwn chord = the notes in the span are one chord. Chord change inside = the span contains a second chord. Merge with next = the chord goes on, or is completed, right after End.
Roman numeralTick every option that is an acceptable reading in the key shown — more than one can be right (for example a triad and its seventh chord). None of these when no option is acceptable.
Beats and spans

Beats are counted in the lower number of the time signature (in 2/2, beat 2 is the third quarter note); the card gives the quarter-note position when it differs. End is musWM’s next reading, or the point where musWM shows no reading, and is not included. Note names are spelled for the local key; the pitches never change.

Raters, and how the answers are used
  • Who rates. Anyone can take part after signing in with an e-mail address and giving a name, country, profession and education. Nothing has to be finished: rate as many positions as you like, in any order, on any device.
  • Saving. Answers are saved to the rater’s account a few seconds after each change and when the page is closed, and come back on the next visit, where the page opens at the first unfinished position.
  • What is public. Every rater’s saved answers, including their notes, are shown on the Compare page with name, country, profession and education. E-mail addresses are never shown.
  • Independence. The author, who developed musWM, is also a rater. Those answers are not independent and are reported separately. Statements about how well an analyser performs rest on independent raters; results are reported per rater, pooled, and with the agreement between raters.
  • Scoring the Roman numerals. A position counts once its Roman-numeral answer is confirmed and not Unclear. An analyser is correct there when an option it gave was ticked; None of these counts all three as wrong. A changed answer counts with its final value; how often, and in which direction, answers were changed after the reveal is reported too.
  • Nothing is filtered. Every rating is published with the results, including those against musWM.
