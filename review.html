Blind review

Check musWM’s notes, root and bass against the score. No Roman numeral or engine label is shown.

How to rate
  1. Each position is one musWM Roman numeral (hidden). From Start to End, blue notes are in musWM’s list and orange ones are not.
  2. Find the chord at Start. Passing, neighbour, suspended and ornamental notes are not chord notes.
  3. Answer the four questions. When you cannot decide, choose Unclear.
NotesExactly = the list is only the chord notes. All + extra = every chord note is there, plus a passing/neighbour note or a note that does not sound. Missing = a chord note is not in the list — an orange note that is a chord note of the chord at Start. Spelling is ignored (G♭ = F♯; say so in the note).
RootIs this the root of the chord at Start?
BassIs this the lowest chord note at Start? If a lower note enters later in the span (broken chord, moving bass), choose Unclear and write “bass X→Y”.
SpanOwn chord = the notes in the span are one chord. Chord change inside = the span contains a second chord. Merge with next = the chord goes on, or is completed, right after End.
Beats, sample and rater

Beats are counted in the lower number of the time signature (in 2/2, beat 2 is the third quarter note); the card gives the quarter-note position when it differs. End is musWM’s next reading, or the point where musWM shows no reading, and is not included. Loading the sample… The rater is the author — a single, non-independent rater; a second rater on the same sample is the next step. Every rating is published with the results, including those against musWM. Only the random part estimates an overall rate.
