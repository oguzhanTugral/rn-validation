Accuracy against the textbook

Each compared position yields one verdict per engine: 1 if its Roman numeral matches the textbook label at that bar and beat, 0 if it does not. An engine’s accuracy is the share of its verdicts that are 1.

All grades come from a single source: the textbook. Each compared cell is fixed at 1 or 0 by the criterion below, on Alignment and Disagreement rows alike, and no grade can be altered by hand.

Preliminary results. The figures on this site are first results. After detailed validation, the final accuracy figures may change by up to ±5 percentage points for AnalysisGNN and AugmentedNet and by about ±1 point for musWM. The margin combines two measured sources: resampling whole works gives a 95% interval of about ±3 points for AnalysisGNN and AugmentedNet and ±0.15 for musWM, and a single change to the normalisation rules has already moved the two learned engines by 4–5 points. It applies to accuracy under the present textbook criterion; comparison with independent expert annotation measures something different and may move all three engines further.
